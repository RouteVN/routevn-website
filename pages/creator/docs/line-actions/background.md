## Background sources

Background can come from 3 different assets:

- Images
- Videos
- Layouts

## Animations

Pick an `Update` or `Transition` animation

### Update

Update animations are used when we keep the same background. Some examples if if you want the background to shake, or have an earthquake or even do a 360 degree rotation. In such cases the background asset remains the same.


### Transition

Use transition animation when you want to switch from one background to another.

## Deleting a background

Right click at background and click delete
