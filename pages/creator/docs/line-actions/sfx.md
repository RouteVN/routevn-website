Sound Effects are similar to BGM, but with a few differences:

- There can be multile sound effects playing at a time
- Sound effects will not loop by default

## SFX source

You can use any sounds you have previously uploaded

## SFX options

- Loop: Whether the BGM playaback should loop or only be one time. Defaults to loop
- Volume: Volume of the BGM
