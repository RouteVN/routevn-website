Sound Effects play one or more sound effects on the current line.

Unlike BGM, multiple sound effects can be added to the same line. Sound effects do not loop by default.

## SFX source

You can use any sound from the [Sounds](/creator/docs/sounds/) page.

## Adding a sound effect

To add a sound effect:

1. Click `Add New Sound Effect`.
2. Select the sound you want to use.
3. Click `Select`.

Use the search field when the sound list is large.

Double click a sound in the selector to preview it in the audio player.

## Editing a sound effect

Click a sound effect card to choose a different sound.

Double click a sound effect card to preview it in the audio player.

## SFX options

Each sound effect has its own Loop and Volume settings.

### Loop

Loop controls whether the sound effect repeats.

Use `Loop` when the sound effect should repeat. Use `Don't Loop` when the sound effect should play once.

Loop is disabled by default.

### Volume

Volume controls how loud the sound effect is.

The range is `0` to `100`, and the default is `50`.

## Removing a sound effect

To remove a sound effect:

1. Right click the sound effect card.
2. Select `Delete`.
