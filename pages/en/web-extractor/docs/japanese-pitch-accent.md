## Install Japanese Pitch Accent

Pitch accent shows the high and low pitch patterns of Japanese words. Mastering these patterns can help you sound more natural when speaking Japanese. It is optional, however, and is not installed during the initial dictionary setup. If you’d like to see pitch accent information during your immersion, follow the instructions below:

1. Open [**Settings**](/en/web-extractor/docs/using-the-dictionary/#open-settings).
2. Under **Dictionary & Language**, turn on **Japanese pitch accent**.
3. In the **Japanese pitch accent** dialog, select **Install**.
4. Wait for the message **Japanese pitch accent is enabled**. The dialog will close once installation succeeds.
5. Open a game on [novelgame.jp](http://novelgame.jp) and [look up a word](/en/web-extractor/docs/getting-started/#look-up-your-first-word). Pitch information will appear when the installed data contains a matching word and reading.

## Troubleshooting

If no pitch accent information appears, make sure **Japanese pitch accent** is turned on in the [**Settings**](/en/web-extractor/docs/using-the-dictionary/#open-settings) and that a Japanese definition dictionary is enabled.

Also, keep in mind that not every word has a matching pitch accent record.
