# Behind RouteVN's New Visual Identity

This is a working outline for the post.

## Introduction

- In the past month, we redid RouteVN's branding assets, including the logo, app icon, and website.
- This post will explain how we did that, and the intent behind the visual identity.
- This is about the brand assets and presentation layer, not a redesign of RouteVN as a product.

## Brand intent

- RouteVN is a software application and a tool.
- Because it is a tool, we want to keep the identity neutral and avoid leaning too strongly into one specific taste.
- At the same time, RouteVN is a tool for visual novels, so it should still feel connected to the visual novel space.
- The black and white colors represent this neutrality.
- The goal is to make RouteVN feel usable by many kinds of creators, not only creators with one specific visual style.

- The old icon attempted to represent the choices in a visual novel.
- It was based on an iconic visual novel UI element.
- The issue was that it looked too much like a generic app UI icon.
- We also tried ideas related to routes, since the RouteVN name includes "Route", but we did not find a strong visual direction there.
- In the end, we decided to use the mascot character in the app icon.
- This better represents what RouteVN is.
- It keeps the neutral black and white colors, while making the icon feel closer to visual novels.
- Many visual novel icons use a character face, so this also makes RouteVN easier to understand at a glance.

## Text logo

[TODO: Add logo iterations and final logo.]

- We had the app icon, but we did not have a full logo yet.
- A text logo is a different design problem from a character illustration.
- It depends more on fonts, typography, spacing, weight, and how the wordmark feels in different contexts.
- At the beginning, I had iconic company logos in mind.
- I wanted the logo to feel simple and recognizable, but still have something unique to RouteVN.
- After searching for a while, we ended up working with a VTuber logo designer.
- VTuber logos are often unique, fun, and expressive.
- That direction felt surprisingly close to what we wanted RouteVN to represent.
- We still kept the final logo simple.
- The designer could probably have made something much more decorative, but we already had the icon and mascot to carry the character side.
- For the logo, we wanted to focus on the text and avoid adding too much noise.
- It took many iterations to try different fonts, font weights, shapes, and balance.
- In the end, we arrived at a text logo that can work on both black and white backgrounds.
- That mattered because the RouteVN identity uses both colors heavily.

## Our mascot character

- Makkuro is the RouteVN mascot character.
- She is a mysterious girl.
- She can take different personalities and act in different settings.
- We do not want to explain everything about her directly. The illustrations and stories around her should reveal more over time.

### Design notes

- `Makkuro` means all black or pitch black.
- To honor that, we made her hair fully black, using `#000000`.
- We also tried to make her outfit fully black, but it was difficult to add readable shading that way.
- Because of that, the outfit had to be a little lighter, so the shadows could still be fully black.
- Another issue with using `#000000` is that the line art is also usually black.
- If the line art and hair were both pure black, the shapes would become hard to read.
- Because of that, the line color is not pure black.

### Working with the illustrator

- Makkuro only became what she is thanks to the illustrator.
- The illustrator was not only drawing what we asked for.
- She also proposed ideas and brought a lot of knowledge about character design and fashion.
- This helped the character feel more complete than a simple mascot brief.

## Main illustration

- We wanted a strong illustration that could be used for cover pages, the website, and other public-facing materials.
- By that point, we already had the character design.
- We decided that the homepage would be mostly the illustration and the logo.
- Besides the character, we needed objects around her so the illustration would not feel too empty.
- We chose objects based on the things we value as a creator-focused brand.
- RouteVN's users are often writers, artists, composers, and other creative people.
- The objects in the illustration represent the things needed to make a visual novel.

### Objects in the illustration

- Drawing paper and a drawing tablet.
- A pen and notebook for writing.
- An electric piano keyboard for music.
- A recording microphone for voice acting.

### Notes on object choices

- We considered adding a computer keyboard for writing.
- We did not use it because a keyboard reads more broadly as "computer" than "writing".
- A pen and notebook felt closer to the act of writing stories.

## Stickers

- We also wanted to make stickers.
- Stickers are fun, cute, easy to share, and easy to reuse in many places.
- They give RouteVN more personality without needing long explanations.
- We can use them in the RouteVN Discord server.
- We can print them as merchandise.
- We can also place them on the website to make pages feel more lively than plain text.
- This gives the brand a more playful touch while still keeping the main product identity simple.

## Website refresh

[TODO: Add website before and after screenshots.]

- Beyond standard layout fixes, we decided to decorate the website with more illustrations.
- We added the stickers in places where the page needed a more friendly touch.
- We also reused object images from the main illustration.
- This helps emphasize what RouteVN values: writing, drawing, music, voice acting, and the creative work behind visual novels.
- The goal was not to make the website feel busy.
- The goal was to make it feel less generic and more connected to RouteVN's identity.

## Homepage direction

- The homepage is intentionally simple.
- There is not much we need to say on that page.
- The goal is for visitors to look at the illustration and understand what RouteVN values.
- The illustration should carry the emotional and creative side.
- The logo should keep the product identity clear.
- Visitors can then click into the actual pages for product information, downloads, documentation, and updates.

## Possible closing

- This refresh is not only about making RouteVN look nicer.
- It is about making the product feel clearer, more intentional, and closer to the creators we want to support.
- RouteVN is still a neutral tool, but it should not feel empty.
- The new visual identity gives RouteVN a face, a tone, and a stronger foundation for future pages, illustrations, and stories.
