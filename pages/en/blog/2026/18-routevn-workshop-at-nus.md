On 3 Sep 2026, we conducted a [RouteVN workshop](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dcytp29z7VO/) at the National University of Singapore (NUS) as part of the [NUS Libraries Collections Game Jam 2026](https://itch.io/jam/nus-libraries-collections-2026).

Instead of a written recap, we created [Makkuro is Visiting NUS](https://routevn.itch.io/makkuro-is-visiting-nus), a visual novel about our experience at the workshop.
