Use this section when you are ready to upload and release your exported build.

Before publishing, make sure you have created and exported a build from the [Versions](/en/creator/docs/versions/) page.

## itch.io

itch.io is a destination for self publishing indie visual novels.

Here is a quick guide to upload a web version:

- When creating the game, in 'Kind of project', choose 'HTML'
