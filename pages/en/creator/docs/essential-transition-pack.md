The [Essential Transition Pack](/en/creator/asset-store/essential-transition-pack/) contains 42 reusable assets for visual transitions.

It includes tween and mask animations with mask images.

To use it, copy the pack's import URL, open `Project > Animations` in Creator, click `Import`, and paste the URL.
