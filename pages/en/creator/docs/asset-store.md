The [RouteVN Creator Asset Store](/en/creator/asset-store/) lists reusable asset packs.

Open a pack to preview its contents and copy its import URL.

## Import a pack

1. Open `Project > Animations` or `Project > Transforms` in Creator.
2. Click `Import`.
3. Paste the pack's import URL.
4. Continue and choose a destination folder when asked.
